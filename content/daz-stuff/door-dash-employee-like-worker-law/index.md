We’re sharing important information about your rights and entitlements as a gig worker engaged by DoorDash.

As a gig worker engaged by DoorDash, you have rights and entitlements under the Fair Work Act 2009 (Cth) (the Act) and Minimum Standards Order.

The MSO sets out the standards that apply to your work, including your minimum earnings with DoorDash for the work that you perform.

Your rights include:

a) Challenging an "unfair deactivation" from a digital platform in the Fair Work Commission if you have been performing work on a regular basis for at least 6 months and earn below the contractor high income threshold;

b) The right under the Minimum Standards Order to be paid no less overall than the earnings floor over any Earnings Period for all Engaged Time.

To find out more about the Minimum Standards Order that applies to you, check here.

You have a right to be a member of a Union.

The Transport Worker’s Union (TWU) is the Union with the right to represent you and provides advice, support and legal representation to gig members including, in relation to the above matters.

You can join and access assistance and representation from the TWU by going to the Transport Workers Union website*.
